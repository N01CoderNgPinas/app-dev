# The Batman (2022): A Movie Review

## A. Introduction

**The Batman** is a dark, detective-driven take on the famous DC Comics hero, directed by *Matt Reeves*. Instead of retelling the origin story, the film follows a young Bruce Wayne in only his second year of fighting crime in Gotham City. It focuses on mystery, investigation, and the emotional cost of vengeance, which makes it feel very different from many other superhero movies. The film's moody visuals and gritty tone give Gotham a rain-soaked, noir atmosphere.

- **Genre:** Superhero, Neo-noir, Crime Thriller
- **Release Year:** 2022
- **Director:** Matt Reeves
- **Runtime:** About 176 minutes

---

## B. Synopsis

### Story Summary

Bruce Wayne, who has spent two years as the vigilante Batman, is trying to clean up the crime-ridden streets of Gotham. On Halloween night, the city's mayor is brutally murdered by a masked killer who calls himself the **Riddler**. The killer leaves behind a cipher addressed to Batman, and the hero teams up with Lieutenant James Gordon to solve the case. As more public officials are targeted, the clues lead Batman into Gotham's criminal underworld, where he meets Selina Kyle, Oswald "Penguin" Cobblepot, and crime boss Carmine Falcone. Batman slowly uncovers a web of corruption that reaches the highest levels of the city, including secrets tied to his own family's legacy. In the climax, the Riddler's plan threatens to flood Gotham and harm thousands of innocent people. Batman realizes that fear and vengeance are not enough, and he decides to become a symbol of **hope** for the city.

### Key Story Themes

1. **Vengeance vs. Justice:** Batman must question whether his methods truly help Gotham.
2. **Corruption:** Powerful people hide terrible secrets behind a respectable image.
3. **Legacy:** Bruce faces the truth about his family's past.
4. **Hope:** The story ends with a message that Gotham needs inspiration, not only fear.

### Character Descriptions

1. **Bruce Wayne / Batman:** A brooding, isolated billionaire who hides in the shadows and treats crime-fighting as a mission. He is still learning how to balance anger with real justice.
2. **Selina Kyle / Catwoman:** A sharp and resourceful waitress and thief who moves through Gotham's underworld. Her search for answers keeps crossing paths with Batman's investigation.
3. **Edward Nashton / The Riddler:** A disturbed, intelligent serial killer who targets corrupt officials and taunts Batman with puzzles. He believes he is exposing the truth about Gotham.
4. **Lt. James Gordon:** One of the few honest cops in Gotham and Batman's trusted ally. He helps Batman access crime scenes and follow leads.
5. **Oswald Cobblepot / The Penguin:** A cunning lieutenant in the Falcone crime family who runs a nightclub called the Iceberg Lounge. He is ambitious and dangerous, with a sharp sense of survival.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *The Batman* because I enjoy stories that mix action with mystery. I was curious to see a version of Batman who acts as a true **detective**, which is a side of the character that is often overlooked in other films.

### Things I Liked

- **The atmosphere and cinematography:** The dark color palette, heavy rain, and shadowy city streets make Gotham feel like a real, living place.
- **The performances:** Robert Pattinson plays a haunted and intense Bruce Wayne, and Paul Dano makes the Riddler genuinely unsettling.
- **The music and sound design:** The score builds tension and gives the film a powerful emotional identity.
- **The mystery plot:** The clues and puzzles keep viewers guessing and make the story feel like a real investigation.

### My Rating

** 9 / 10**

### Recommendation

> *The Batman* proves that a superhero story can be a serious crime thriller with real emotional depth.

I **highly recommend** this movie to viewers who like mysteries, crime stories, and character-focused drama. It is long and fairly dark, so people who prefer light, fast-paced superhero adventures may find it slow. However, anyone who is patient and enjoys careful storytelling will find it very rewarding.
